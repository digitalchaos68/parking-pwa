Tips for Keeping Your Car Safe in a Parking Lot

Parking your car in a public lot comes with risks. Theft, vandalism, and break-ins are unfortunately common. In fact, the National Insurance Crime Bureau reports that over 700,000 vehicles are stolen annually in the U.S. alone. By following these simple security practices, you can significantly reduce the chances of becoming a victim.

1. Lock Your Doors

Always lock your car, even if you're just stepping away for a minute. Modern key fobs can fail due to signal interference or dead batteries, so double-check by pulling the handle. In cities like Chicago and Los Angeles, unlocked cars are often targeted for "smash-and-grab" thefts.

2. Close All Windows and Sunroof

Never leave any windows cracked. A small opening is enough for a thief to reach in with a coat hanger or tool and unlock the door. This is a common tactic used in crowded parking garages.

3. Hide Valuables

Put bags, electronics, and packages in the trunk or out of sight before arriving. A visible item is a target. According to AAA, cars with visible valuables are 3x more likely to be broken into.

4. Park in Well-Lit Areas

Choose spots near building entrances, security cameras, or under bright lights. Avoid dark corners and isolated areas. Well-lit lots have up to 50% fewer break-ins, according to police data.

5. Engage the Parking Brake

Especially on hills, the parking brake prevents your car from rolling if a tire is tampered with. It also makes it harder for thieves to tow your vehicle silently.

6. Use Anti-Theft Devices

Steering wheel locks, gear shift locks, or alarm systems can deter thieves. Even a simple "Klaxon" horn can scare off an intruder. For high-risk areas, consider a GPS tracker.

Use ParkHere to save your GPS location and take a photo of your surroundings. This helps you find your car quickly and can serve as evidence if needed. You can even share your spot with family for safe pickup.

Pro Tips for Extra Security

  • Never leave spare keys in the car (e.g., under the wheel well).
  • Remove garage door openers from view.
  • Use tinted windows to obscure the interior.
  • Install a dashcam that records when parked.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if my car is stolen? Report it immediately to the police and your insurance. Provide your ParkHere photo and location for evidence.

Are parking garages safer than street parking? Generally yes — garages have more lighting and surveillance, but choose upper levels with foot traffic.
