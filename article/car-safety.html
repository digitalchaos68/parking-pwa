Tips for Keeping Your Car Safe in a Parking Lot

Parking your car in a public lot comes with risks. Theft, vandalism, and break-ins are unfortunately common. By following these simple security practices, you can significantly reduce the chances of becoming a victim.

1. Lock Your Doors

Always lock your car, even if you're just stepping away for a minute. Modern key fobs can fail, so double-check.

2. Close All Windows and Sunroof

Never leave any windows cracked. A small opening is enough for a thief to reach in and unlock the door.

3. Hide Valuables

Put bags, electronics, and packages in the trunk or out of sight. A visible item is a target.

4. Park in Well-Lit Areas

Choose spots near building entrances, security cameras, or under bright lights. Avoid dark corners and isolated areas.

5. Engage the Parking Brake

Especially on hills, the parking brake prevents your car from rolling if a tire is tampered with.

6. Use Anti-Theft Devices

Steering wheel locks, gear shift locks, or alarm systems can deter thieves.

7. Save Your Spot with ParkHere

Use ParkHere to save your GPS location and take a photo of your surroundings. This helps you find your car and can serve as evidence if needed.
