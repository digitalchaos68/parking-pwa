How to Find Free or Cheap Parking Spots

Parking fees can add up quickly, especially in cities and airports. With a little planning, you can find free or cheap spots and save money. Here’s how.

1. Check for Free Time Windows

Many cities offer free parking after 6 PM or on weekends. Always check the signs.

2. Look for Residential Zones

On weekends or holidays, residential areas often have no restrictions. Be respectful of residents.

3. Use Parking Apps

Apps like ParkHere can show real-time availability and highlight free or low-cost options.

4. Park at Public Transit Hubs

Many train or bus stations have free or low-cost parking. Take public transit the rest of the way.

5. Shop at Malls with Free Parking

Some malls offer free parking for customers. Check their website or call ahead.

6. Avoid Peak Hours

Parking is often cheaper or free during off-peak hours. Plan your trip accordingly.

7. Walk a Little Further

Spots farther from your destination are often free or cheaper. A 10-minute walk can save you $10.
