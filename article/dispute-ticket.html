How to Dispute a Parking Ticket

Not all parking tickets are valid. If you believe you were wrongly fined, you have the right to dispute it. Here’s how to fight a parking ticket successfully.

1. Review the Ticket

Check the violation code, date, time, and location. Look for errors in the license plate number or vehicle description.

2. Know the Deadline

Most cities require you to dispute within 10-30 days. Missing the deadline means you must pay.

3. Gather Evidence

Photos of the parking signs, your vehicle's position, or a receipt showing you were elsewhere can be powerful.

4. Submit Online

Many cities allow you to dispute online through their official portal. Upload your evidence and write a clear explanation.

5. Attend a Hearing

If your dispute is denied, you may request a hearing. Be polite and stick to the facts.

6. Common Valid Reasons

Incorrect meter, obscured signs, vehicle was moved, or emergency situation.

7. Prevention

Use ParkHere to save a photo of the parking signs and your spot, which can serve as evidence if needed.
