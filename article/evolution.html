The Evolution of Parking from Meters to Mobile Apps

Parking has evolved dramatically over the past century. From simple metal meters to smartphone apps and self-driving cars, the way we park is a reflection of technological progress. Let’s take a journey through the history of parking.

1. The Horse Carriage Era (1800s)

There were no formal parking rules. Horses were tied to posts, and space was abundant.

2. The First Parking Meters (1935)

The first parking meter was installed in Oklahoma City. It charged 5 cents for 30 minutes.

3. The Rise of the Automobile (1950s)

As car ownership exploded, cities introduced time limits, no-parking zones, and towing.

4. Multi-Space Meters (1980s)

Single machines replaced individual meters, allowing payment for multiple spots.

5. Digital Meters & Pay-by-Phone (2000s)

Touchscreen meters and phone payments reduced the need for coins.

6. Smart Parking & Sensors (2010s)

Garages used sensors to show real-time availability. Apps began to emerge.

7. Mobile Apps & PWAs (2020s)

Apps like ParkHere let you save your spot, share it, and get directions — no installation needed.

8. The Future: Autonomous Parking (2030s)

Self-driving cars will park themselves in robotic garages, optimizing space and reducing congestion.
