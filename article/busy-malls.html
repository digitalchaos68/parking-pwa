The Ultimate Guide to Parking in Busy Malls

Parking at a busy mall can be overwhelming, especially during holidays or weekends. With the right strategy, you can find a spot quickly and remember exactly where you parked. Here’s your ultimate guide.

1. Arrive Early or Late

The busiest times are usually 11 AM to 3 PM. Arrive before 10 AM or after 4 PM for better availability.

2. Park on the Outer Edges

The spots farthest from the entrance are often the first to fill, but they're also the first to open up as people leave.

3. Use the Upper or Lower Levels

Ground level fills up fast. Head to the top or bottom floors for more open spaces.

4. Look for Service Entrances

Some malls have secondary entrances for deliveries or staff. These areas often have less crowded parking.

5. Save Your Spot with ParkHere

Use ParkHere to save your exact GPS location and add a photo of your surroundings. No more walking in circles!

6. Note Nearby Landmarks

Look for unique signs, columns, or floor markings. "I parked near the red column on Level 3."

7. Share Your Spot

If you're shopping with family, share your parking spot via QR code or link so everyone can find the car.
