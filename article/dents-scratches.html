Dealing with Minor Dents and Scratches in a Parking Lot

Minor dents and scratches are common in busy parking lots. Whether it's a door ding, a scrape from a shopping cart, or a bump from another car, knowing how to handle the situation can save you time, money, and stress.

1. Stay Calm

It's frustrating, but getting upset won't help. Take a deep breath and assess the damage calmly.

2. Document the Damage

Take clear photos of the dent or scratch from multiple angles. Include shots of the surrounding area to show context.

3. Look for the Responsible Party

If the incident just happened, try to locate the other driver. If they've left, check for witnesses or security cameras.

4. File a Police Report (If Significant)

For larger damage, file a report with local police. This is often required for insurance claims.

5. Contact Your Insurance

Report the incident to your insurer. If you have comprehensive coverage, it may cover the repair, but weigh the cost against your deductible.

6. Get Repair Estimates

Visit a body shop for an estimate. For small dents, paintless dent repair (PDR) is often fast and affordable.

7. Prevention

Choose wider spots when possible, use ParkHere to save a photo of your surroundings, and consider parking near pillars or at the end of a row.
