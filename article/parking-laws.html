Understanding Parking Laws and Regulations

Parking laws are designed to keep traffic flowing, ensure safety, and manage limited space. While they vary by city and country, many rules are universal. Understanding them can save you from fines and towing.

1. No Parking Zones

Areas marked with red lines or "No Parking" signs are off-limits at all times. This includes fire hydrants, intersections, and bus stops.

2. Time Restrictions

Yellow or white lines often indicate time-limited parking. Always check the posted signs for hours and duration.

3. Handicap Parking

These spots are reserved for drivers with disabilities. Unauthorized use can result in heavy fines and towing.

4. Residential Permits

In some neighborhoods, only residents with permits can park during certain hours. Look for signs indicating permit zones.

5. Loading Zones

These are for commercial vehicles only and are often time-limited. Personal vehicles are not allowed.

6. Towing Zones

If your car is parked illegally, it may be towed at your expense. Always check for "Tow Away" signs.

7. Overnight Parking

Some cities prohibit overnight street parking to allow for street cleaning. Check local ordinances.
