A Driver's Guide to Parking Tickets and Fines

Parking tickets are a common frustration for drivers. Whether you forgot the time limit or parked in a restricted zone, understanding the rules can help you avoid fines. Here’s what you need to know.

1. Common Violations

Exceeding time limits, parking in no-parking zones, blocking driveways, or failing to pay at a meter are the most common reasons for tickets.

2. How Fines Are Issued

Tickets are issued by parking enforcement officers or automated systems like cameras. The amount depends on the violation and location.

3. Where to Pay

Pay online, at a kiosk, or by mail. Many cities offer apps to pay and contest tickets.

4. Late Fees

Unpaid tickets accrue late fees and can lead to registration holds or towing.

5. Impact on Insurance

Parking tickets usually don’t affect your insurance, but unpaid fines can lead to license suspension.

6. Record Keeping

Keep a record of paid tickets in case of disputes.

7. Prevention

Use apps like ParkHere to set reminders and save your spot with a photo of the signs.
