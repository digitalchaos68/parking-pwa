Tips for Parking a Large Vehicle (Truck, SUV)

Parking a large vehicle like a truck or SUV requires extra care and space. With the right techniques, you can avoid dings and park confidently in any lot. Here’s how.

1. Choose a Larger Spot

Look for end spots or handicap spaces (if you qualify). They offer more room to maneuver.

2. Measure Your Vehicle

Know your vehicle’s length and width. This helps you judge if a spot is big enough.

3. Use Your Side Mirrors

Adjust them to see the full width of your vehicle. This helps you stay centered in the lane.

4. Back In Slowly

Go slow and use small steering adjustments. It’s easier to correct a small mistake than a big one.

5. Leave Extra Space

Give at least 2-3 feet on each side. This prevents door dings and makes it easier to exit.

6. Use Parking Sensors or Camera

If your vehicle has them, use parking sensors or a rearview camera to judge distance.

7. Ask for Help

If you’re unsure, ask a passenger or passerby to guide you in.
