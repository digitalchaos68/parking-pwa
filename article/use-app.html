How to Use a Parking App Effectively

Parking apps like ParkHere can make your life much easier, but only if you use them correctly. Here’s how to get the most out of your parking app.

1. Save Your Spot Immediately

Tap "Save My Parking Spot" as soon as you park. Don't wait — you might forget the exact location.

2. Add a Photo

Take a photo of your parking spot and the surrounding signs. This helps you identify the spot later.

3. Share with Family or Friends

If you're shopping with others, share your spot via QR code or link so everyone can find the car.

4. Use Voice Guidance

Tap "Find My Car" to get voice-guided directions back to your vehicle. No need to look at your phone.

5. Check Nearby Places

Use the "Find Nearby Places" feature to locate restaurants, cafes, supermarkets, and gas stations.

6. Set a Timer

If you're in a paid zone, set a timer to remind you when to return.

7. Reset When You Leave

Tap "Reset Parking Spot" when you drive away. This clears the data and prepares the app for next time.
