Navigating Parking Fees and Hidden Costs

When you park your car, the meter fee is just the beginning. Hidden costs like fines, towing, and time penalties can quickly turn a simple errand into an expensive ordeal. Here’s how to avoid these costly surprises.

1. Expired Meters

Even a few minutes over can result in a $50+ fine. Use apps like ParkHere to set a timer and get a reminder.

2. Towing Fees

If your car is towed, you’ll pay not only the fine but also a $100–$300 towing fee and daily storage charges.

3. Wrong Zone Parking

Parking in a residential permit zone without authorization can lead to immediate towing and hefty fines.

4. Time Limit Violations

Some areas have strict 2-hour limits enforced by cameras. Exceeding the limit, even by minutes, can result in a ticket.

5. Payment System Errors

App glitches or meter malfunctions don’t excuse non-payment. Always get a digital receipt or take a photo of the confirmation.

6. Event-Based Pricing

During concerts or sports games, dynamic pricing can spike rates. Check the app before you park.

7. International Travel

Rental cars in Europe often come with automated parking fines that are billed to your credit card weeks later, sometimes with high service fees.
