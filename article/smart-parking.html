What is a Smart Parking System?

Smart parking systems use technology to make parking more efficient, reduce congestion, and improve the driver experience. These systems are becoming a key part of smart city infrastructure.

1. Sensor-Based Detection

Ground sensors or cameras detect whether a parking spot is occupied or available in real time.

2. Mobile App Integration

Drivers use apps to see available spots, navigate to them, and even pay for parking.

3. Data Analytics

Cities use parking data to identify trends, optimize pricing, and plan future infrastructure.

4. Dynamic Pricing

Prices can change based on demand, encouraging turnover in busy areas.

5. Reduced Emissions

By reducing the time drivers spend searching for parking, smart systems lower fuel consumption and pollution.

6. Enforcement Efficiency

Automated systems can flag expired meters or unauthorized parking, reducing the need for patrols.

7. User Experience

Drivers save time and stress, knowing exactly where to go and how much to pay.
