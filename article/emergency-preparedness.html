Emergency Preparedness: What to Do in a Parking Lot Incident

Parking lots can be the scene of unexpected incidents — from minor fender benders to medical emergencies. Being prepared can make all the difference in how you respond. Here’s how to stay safe and ready for anything.

1. Build an Emergency Kit

Keep a kit in your car with essentials: flashlight, first aid supplies, jumper cables, blanket, water, and non-perishable snacks.

2. Know Your Location

Use apps like ParkHere to save your exact GPS coordinates. This is crucial if you need to call for help.

3. In Case of an Accident

Check for injuries, call 911 if needed, exchange information, and take photos of the scene and damage.

4. Medical Emergency

If someone collapses or has a medical issue, call 911 immediately. Provide clear location details and stay with the person until help arrives.

5. Car Trouble

If your car won't start, use jumper cables or call roadside assistance. Stay in your car if you feel unsafe.

6. Stay Visible

At night, turn on hazard lights and use a flashlight to signal for help.

7. Share Your Plans

Let someone know where you're going and when you expect to return. Use the WhatsApp reminder feature in ParkHere to automate this.
