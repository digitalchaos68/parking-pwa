The Safest Place to Park Your Car at Night

Parking at night requires extra caution. Whether you're returning late from work or leaving a movie, choosing the right spot can make a big difference in your safety and the security of your vehicle.

1. Choose Well-Lit Areas

Always park under bright lights. Avoid dark corners, covered areas with poor lighting, or spots blocked by trees.

2. Stay Close to Entrances

Park near building entrances, security desks, or busy sidewalks. The more foot traffic, the safer the area.

3. Look for Security Cameras

Spots under surveillance cameras are less likely to be targeted by criminals.

4. Avoid Isolated Spots

Don’t park at the far end of a lot or in areas with limited visibility. Stay where others are parking.

5. Use Designated Safe Zones

Some lots have "safe walk" zones with escorts or emergency call boxes. Use them if available.

6. Check for Obstructions

Make sure your car isn’t blocked by other vehicles and that you have a clear path to the exit.

7. Trust Your Instincts

If a spot feels unsafe, it probably is. Drive around and find a better location.
