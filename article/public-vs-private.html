The Difference Between Public and Private Parking Lots

Not all parking lots are the same. Public and private lots have different rules, enforcement, and risks. Understanding the difference can help you avoid fines and towing.

1. Ownership

Public lots are owned by the city or government. Private lots are owned by businesses, malls, or individuals.

2. Rules and Signs

Public lots follow city ordinances. Private lots can set their own rules, which may be less visible.

3. Enforcement

Public lots are enforced by city officers. Private lots may use private security or towing companies.

4. Towing Policies

Private lot owners have broad rights to tow vehicles that violate their rules, even if the car is legally parked.

5. Cost

Public lots often have lower rates. Private lots may charge more but offer better security or location.

6. Liability

In public lots, the city has limited liability. In private lots, the owner may be liable for damage if security is inadequate.

7. How to Stay Safe

Always read the signs. If in doubt, assume it's private property. Take a photo of the rules with ParkHere.
