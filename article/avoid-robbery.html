How to Avoid a Parking Lot Robbery

Parking lots, especially at night, can be prime locations for robberies. Isolated areas, poor lighting, and distracted drivers make them attractive to criminals. By following these safety tips, you can significantly reduce your risk.

1. Stay Alert and Aware

Look around as you walk to and from your car. Avoid distractions like your phone or headphones.

2. Park in Well-Lit, Busy Areas

Choose spots near building entrances, security desks, or under bright lights. Avoid dark corners and empty sections.

3. Walk Confidently

Walk with purpose, keys in hand, and be ready to get in your car quickly. Criminals often target those who appear vulnerable.

4. Use the "Key in Fist" Technique

Hold your car key between your fingers like a makeshift weapon. It can deter an attacker and provide defense.

5. Check Your Back Seat Before Entering

Before getting in, quickly glance into the back seat to ensure no one is hiding.

6. Lock Your Car Remotely

Unlock your car only when you're close. Keep the door locked until you're inside.

7. Trust Your Instincts

If something feels wrong, don't hesitate to go back inside, call for help, or ask for an escort.
