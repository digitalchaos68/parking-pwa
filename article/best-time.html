The Best Time of Day to Find Parking in Your City

Parking availability changes dramatically throughout the day. By understanding these patterns, you can avoid the stress of circling the block. Here’s when to find the best spots.

1. Early Morning (6 AM - 8 AM)

This is often the best time to find street parking. Most people haven't left for work yet.

2. Late Morning (10 AM - 12 PM)

Street parking becomes scarce as people arrive at work. Look for garages or lots.

3. Midday (12 PM - 2 PM)

Lunchtime brings a surge in parking demand. Expect competition near restaurants and malls.

4. Afternoon (2 PM - 4 PM)

Some spots open up as people leave for appointments or errands.

5. Evening (6 PM - 8 PM)

After work hours see a second wave of demand. However, many residential zones become available.

6. Late Evening (8 PM - 10 PM)

Most commercial areas have plenty of spots. Use apps to find open garages.

7. Weekends

Saturday mornings are busy. Sundays are often the best day for easy parking.
