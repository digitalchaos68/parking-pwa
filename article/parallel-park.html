How to Parallel Park Like a Pro

Parallel parking can be intimidating, but with the right technique, anyone can master it. Here’s a step-by-step guide to help you park like a pro.

Step 1: Find a Suitable Spot

Look for a space that’s at least 1.5 times the length of your car. Turn on your hazard lights to signal your intent.

Step 2: Position Your Car

Align your car with the vehicle in front of the spot, leaving about 2 feet of space.

Step 3: Reverse Slowly

Turn the steering wheel sharply to the right and begin reversing. When your rear bumper is even with the front car’s rear bumper, turn the wheel sharply to the left.

Step 4: Straighten Out

Continue reversing until your car is straight and centered in the space. Adjust as needed.
