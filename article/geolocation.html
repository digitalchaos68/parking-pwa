Using Geolocation for Real-Time Parking Availability

Geolocation is the backbone of modern parking apps. By combining GPS, cellular data, and sensors, apps like ParkHere can show you real-time parking availability and guide you to the nearest open spot.

1. GPS Tracking

Your phone's GPS provides your exact location, allowing the app to find nearby parking options.

2. Sensor Data

Smart parking lots use ground sensors to detect whether a spot is occupied or available.

3. Crowd-Sourced Information

When users save their parking spot, that data contributes to a live map of availability.

4. Real-Time Updates

The app updates every few seconds, so you always have the most current information.

5. Route Optimization

Based on your location and destination, the app suggests the best parking option.

6. Offline Functionality

As a PWA, ParkHere can work offline, using cached location data to guide you back to your car.

7. Privacy

All location data is stored only on your device, not on our servers.
