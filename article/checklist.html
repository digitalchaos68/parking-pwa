A Checklist for Parking Your Car Safely

Parking your car seems simple, but skipping a step can lead to theft, damage, or fines. Use this complete checklist every time you park to ensure your car is safe and secure.

1. Lock Your Car

Always lock your doors, even if you're just stepping away for a minute.

2. Close All Windows and Sunroof

Open windows invite rain, dust, and thieves. Make sure everything is closed.

3. Hide Valuables

Put bags, electronics, and packages in the trunk or out of sight. A visible item is a target.

4. Engage the Parking Brake

Especially on hills, the parking brake prevents your car from rolling.

5. Turn Off Lights and Accessories

Leaving headlights or interior lights on can drain your battery overnight.

6. Check for Leaks or Damage

Look under your car for fluid leaks. Check tires for low pressure or damage.

7. Note Your Location

Use ParkHere to save your GPS location and take a photo of your surroundings.
