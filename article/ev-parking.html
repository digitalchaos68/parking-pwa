The Impact of Electric Vehicles on Parking Infrastructure

As electric vehicles (EVs) become more popular, parking infrastructure is evolving to meet their unique needs. From charging stations to dedicated spots, cities and businesses are adapting to support the EV revolution.

1. Dedicated EV Parking Spots

Many lots now have reserved spots for EVs, often near building entrances or with charging stations.

2. Charging Stations

Level 2 and DC fast chargers are being installed in parking garages, malls, and workplaces.

3. Time Limits

EV spots often have time limits to prevent "charge theft" and ensure availability.

4. Pricing Models

Some stations charge by time, others by kWh. Apps like ParkHere can help you find free or low-cost charging.

5. Urban Planning

Cities are integrating EV charging into new developments and retrofitting existing infrastructure.

6. Home Charging

For those with garages, home charging is the most convenient option, reducing the need for public spots.

7. The Future

Expect more wireless charging, battery-swapping stations, and smart systems that guide EVs to available chargers.
