7 Common Parking Mistakes to Avoid

Parking seems simple, but small mistakes can lead to fines, dings, or even your car being towed. Here are 7 common parking errors and how to avoid them.

1. Parking Too Close to a Fire Hydrant

Never park within 15 feet of a fire hydrant. It's illegal and can result in a fine or towing.

2. Ignoring Time Restrictions

Always check for signs with specific hours. Some zones are free after 7 PM, but others require payment 24/7.

3. Blocking Driveways or Sidewalks

Even if the driveway is empty, blocking it is illegal and inconsiderate.

4. Parking in a No-Parking Zone

Double yellow lines mean no waiting at any time. Single yellow lines have time restrictions.

5. Not Using Your Parking Brake

On hills, failing to engage the parking brake can cause your car to roll.

6. Parking Under Trees

Bird droppings, sap, and falling branches can damage your paint and windshield.

7. Forgetting to Lock Your Car

Always lock your car and hide valuables. It only takes a few seconds for a thief to break in.
