Your Rights and Responsibilities in a Parking Lot

Parking lots are shared spaces with rules for everyone’s safety and convenience. Understanding your rights and responsibilities can prevent conflicts and costly mistakes.

1. Right to a Safe Spot

You have the right to park in a spot that doesn’t block traffic, fire lanes, or accessible spaces.

2. Responsibility to Park Properly

Don’t straddle lines or park too close to other cars. Leave enough space for doors to open safely.

3. Private Property Rules

Private lot owners can set their own rules (e.g., "Customers Only") and tow vehicles that violate them.

4. Accident Responsibility

If you damage another car while opening your door or maneuvering, you are responsible, even in a parking lot.

5. Security Expectations

Public lots have limited security. Private lots may be liable if poor lighting or lack of cameras leads to theft.

6. Overnight Parking

Most lots prohibit overnight parking. Check signs or ask management before leaving your car overnight.

7. Reporting Issues

If you see a damaged car, suspicious activity, or a safety hazard, report it to the lot manager or security.
