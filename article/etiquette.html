Parking Etiquette: Rules Every Driver Should Know

Parking etiquette is about being respectful and considerate of other drivers. Following these unwritten rules makes parking safer and more pleasant for everyone.

1. Don't Hog the Spot

Don't park so far into your spot that you block the car next to you. Leave enough space for them to open their doors safely.

2. Don't Block the Driveway

Even if the driveway is empty, blocking it is illegal and inconsiderate. Wait until it's clear.

3. Don't Park in Handicap Spots Without a Permit

These spots are for people with disabilities. Misusing them can result in fines and towing.

4. Don't Leave Your Car Running

Don't leave your car unattended with the engine running. It's a security risk and against the law in many places.

5. Don't Park Too Close to Other Cars

Leave at least 1-2 feet on each side to prevent door dings and make it easier to exit.

6. Don't Leave Trash in the Lot

Take your trash with you. Littering is disrespectful and harms the environment.

7. Be Patient and Courteous

If someone is leaving a spot, wait patiently. Don't honk or try to cut them off.
