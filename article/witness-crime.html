What to Do If You Witness a Crime in a Parking Lot

Parking lots can be the scene of crimes ranging from theft and vandalism to assault. If you witness a crime, your actions can make a crucial difference. Here’s how to respond safely and responsibly.

1. Ensure Your Safety First

Do not intervene directly. Your safety is the top priority. Move to a safe distance or back into your vehicle.

2. Call 911 Immediately

Report the incident to the police. Provide your location, a description of what you saw, and any details about the suspect or vehicle.

3. Observe and Remember

Note the suspect's appearance, clothing, direction of travel, and any vehicle details like make, model, color, and license plate.

4. Use Your Phone Discreetly

If safe, use your smartphone to record video or take photos. This evidence can be invaluable to law enforcement.

5. Stay at the Scene (If Safe)

Police may need to speak with you. Wait in a secure location until they arrive.

6. Provide a Statement

Give a clear, factual account of what you witnessed. Avoid speculation or assumptions.

7. Follow Up

If you have evidence, share it with the police. You may be contacted later as part of the investigation.
